Your concerns answered

Still on the fence?

Every question a first-time booker has — answered directly.

"What if guests think it is strange?"

They won't. Within thirty seconds they'll be switching channels and laughing. Silent disco is the one thing guests still talk about years later.

"What if it is complicated to set up?"

One transmitter per channel. One cable per transmitter. One button press. Operational in under ten minutes, and we've included a step-by-step video so you'll feel ready before the night.

"What if something stops working on the night?"

Email Todd directly at booking@shushpartyhire.com.au. Not a help desk. A real local who replies fast and talks you through it. Most issues resolve in minutes.

"What if my venue has a noise curfew?"

That's precisely why people choose a silent disco. Music stays inside the headsets. The only sound your neighbours hear is conversation and laughter.

"What if I need more headsets than I booked?"

Contact us as early as possible. We carry up to 400 headsets. Our recommendation is always one headset per guest — nothing takes the shine off a party like someone left out.

"What if my guests are older or not tech-savvy?"

Three buttons: power, volume, channel. That's the entire learning curve. Ryman Retirement Village in Ocean Grove is among our past clients. Every generation manages it.

Our story

Born in the Greek Islands.

Years before Shush existed, Todd was running a silent disco on the Greek Islands. Night after night, strangers became friends and quiet venues turned electric — not a single speaker in earshot.

What struck him most was how it united people. Three different channels meant three different worlds, yet everyone was dancing together under the same stars.

When Todd returned to Geelong, he brought that vision home. Shush was built on one idea: the atmosphere he created in the Greek Islands should be available to anyone on the Bellarine Peninsula.
