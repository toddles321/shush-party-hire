The experience

The moment they put the headphones on, everything changes.

One person is singing along to '90s pop. The person beside them is lost in house music. Someone at the back has just discovered they love hip-hop. From the outside, they are all dancing together in near-silence — which is somehow the funniest, most joyful thing you will ever see at a party.

That is a silent disco. And it works anywhere speakers cannot go. Winery lawns, holiday houses, beach events, rooftops, backyards, aged care common rooms, school halls — wherever noise is a problem, the music plays on through the headsets.

Three channels means three different music experiences happening simultaneously. Every guest finds their sound. Every generation joins the same dance floor.

Three channels explained

One Room. Three Dance Floors.

Our headsets broadcast three simultaneous music channels. Guests switch with a button — and the LED colour shows which channel they are on.

Channel One — Red

Your first playlist or DJ broadcasts here. Every guest with a red LED is hearing the same song at the same moment.

Channel Two — Green

A completely different genre on a second transmitter. Guests switch with one press of a button on the headset.

Channel Three — Blue

A third independent source. Hip-hop, country, throwbacks — every taste covered without compromise.

All three transmitters are included in every Shush hire — at no extra cost.

Your concerns answered

Still on the Fence?

Every question a first-time booker has — answered directly.

"What if guests think it is strange?"

They will not. Within thirty seconds of putting the headsets on, your guests will be switching channels and laughing. Silent disco is the party feature people talk about for years.

"What if it is complicated to set up?"

One transmitter per channel. One cable per transmitter. One button press. Operational in under ten minutes, and we include a step-by-step video so you are confident before the night.

"What if something stops working on the night?"

Call Todd directly on 0400 050 176. Not a help desk. A real local who picks up and talks you through it. Most issues resolve in two minutes.

"What if my venue has a noise curfew?"

That is precisely why people choose a silent disco. Music stays inside the headsets. The only sound your neighbours hear is conversation and laughter.

"What if I need more headsets than I booked?"

Contact us as early as possible. We carry up to 400 headsets. Our recommendation is always one headset per guest — nothing takes the shine off a party like someone left out.

"What if my guests are older or not tech-savvy?"

Three buttons: power, volume, channel. That is the entire learning curve. Ryman Retirement Village in Ocean Grove is among our past clients. Every generation manages it.

Our story

Born in the Greek Islands

Years before Shush existed, our founder Todd was running a silent disco club on the Greek Islands. Night after night, he watched strangers become friends and quiet venues transform into something electric — all without a single speaker disturbing the surrounding streets.

What struck Todd most was how the experience united people. Three different channels meant three different worlds, yet everyone was dancing together under the same stars.

When Todd returned to Geelong, he brought that vision home. Shush was built on one idea: the atmosphere he created in the Greek Islands should be available to anyone on the Bellarine Peninsula.
