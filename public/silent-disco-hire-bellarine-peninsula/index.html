Frequently asked questions

How much does silent disco hire cost?

Packages start from $99 for 10 headsets for one night, and 50 headsets is $319 (about $6.38 per guest). Two to three nights adds 50%, a week adds 100%. Delivery is a flat $40 anywhere in our zone, or pickup from Point Lonsdale is free. All prices include GST.

How do I book and what is the deposit?

Choose your date and headset count in the live quote tool, then pay a $150 deposit to lock the date in. The balance is due before your event, and your confirmation is emailed straight away.

How many music channels are there?

Three. Headsets show red, green or blue LEDs so guests can see which channel they are on, and all three transmitters are included in every hire at no extra cost.

How long do the headsets last and how far does the signal reach?

Battery life is 10 to 12 hours and the signal range is up to 500m. Transmitters work through interior walls and floors.

Can I collect the headsets myself?

Yes. Pickup from Point Lonsdale is free. Up to 200 headsets fit in a standard car.
