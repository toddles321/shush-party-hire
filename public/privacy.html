Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Shush Party Hire ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. Who We Are

Shush Party Hire is a silent disco equipment hire business based in Point Lonsdale, Victoria, Australia. We operate at shushpartyhire.com.au. Our ABN is 81 343 920 567.

Questions about this policy: booking@shushpartyhire.com.au

2. Information We Collect

When you make a booking or contact us, we collect:

  • Contact details — name, email address, and phone number
  • Event details — event date, type, delivery address, and headset quantity
  • Payment information — we do not store card details. All payments are processed securely by Stripe. We receive confirmation and a booking reference only.

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, device type, referring URL (via Google Analytics 4)
  • Advertising data — whether you visited after seeing an ad, and actions on the site (via Meta Pixel)
  • Cookies — small files stored in your browser to improve your experience and measure advertising effectiveness

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and confirm your booking
  • Send your equipment hire agreement and event reminders
  • Contact you about your booking via email, SMS, or phone
  • Process your payment and refund your bond after the event
  • Request a Google review after your event
  • Improve our website and measure advertising effectiveness

We will not use your information for any unrelated purpose without your consent.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We share your information only with the third-party services we use to operate:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Airtable — booking database
  • HubSpot — customer relationship management
  • Google — website analytics (GA4) and advertising
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — advertising and remarketing
  • ClickSend — SMS notifications

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

5. Data Retention

Booking information is retained for up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes, as required by Australian law. Website analytics data is retained for up to 26 months. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time (see Section 7).

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for analytics (Google Analytics 4) and advertising (Meta Pixel). You can opt out of Google Analytics via Google's opt-out tool. Manage advertising preferences at Facebook Ad Preferences. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies in settings.

7. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at booking@shushpartyhire.com.au and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

8. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS. Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). Access to our booking systems is protected by passwords and two-factor authentication.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available at shushpartyhire.com.au/privacy.html.
