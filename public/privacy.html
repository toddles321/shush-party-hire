Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who We Are

Shush Party Hire is a silent disco headset hire business operating in Geelong and the Bellarine Peninsula, Victoria, Australia. We are operated by Todd van Berkel (ABN available on request). Contact: booking@shushpartyhire.com.au | 0400 050 176.

2. Information We Collect

When you make a booking we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your event date, suburb, and event details
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store card details)

We also collect anonymised website usage data via Google Analytics and may use Facebook Pixel to measure advertising effectiveness.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and manage your booking
  • To contact you about your hire (delivery times, event details)
  • To send a booking confirmation email
  • To improve our website and marketing

4. Data Storage

Booking records are stored in Airtable (USA-based, GDPR-compliant). Payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Cookies

We use cookies for Google Analytics (traffic analysis) and Facebook Pixel (ad measurement). You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing booking@shushpartyhire.com.au. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email booking@shushpartyhire.com.au or call 0400 050 176.
