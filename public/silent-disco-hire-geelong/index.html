Why Geelong events choose a silent disco

Music stays inside the headphones, so a silent disco suits places where speakers cause problems: backyards with close neighbours, noise-curfew venues, halls, and anywhere you want guests to talk as easily as they dance. Three channels mean everyone finds a genre they love, which makes it work for mixed-age crowds.

What is included

  • Headsets with three LED channels (red, green, blue) and individual volume control
  • All three transmitters, at no extra cost
  • 10 to 12 hours of battery and up to 500m signal range
  • A 45-second setup video and written step-by-step guide so you can run the night yourself
  • Public liability insurance held
